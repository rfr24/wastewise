Why WasteWise?

We’ve all experienced it: waste sorting at Evans Hall can be confusing! Different bins, inconsistent signage, and uncertainty about what actually gets recycled or composted make it harder to do the right thing.

As your 1Y Sustainability Chair, I built WasteWise to make sorting simpler for students — and to better understand where our waste systems could improve.

The goal is twofold: help students make informed decisions in the moment, and use anonymous patterns in their questions to identify opportunities for clearer signage, better education, and operational improvements.
